# HC-OS V1 — Milestone H: OctopusMind doctrine (Lumen closure tightening)

**Source:** OctopusMind, aligned to **Lumen closure record** (Milestone H soft pass / passable with watchpoints, 2026-03-22).

**Role:** **Gate 1** — structural admissibility, insertion/suppression boundaries, H/E conflict, kill-switch logic. Pairs with **`docs/HC_OS_V1_Milestone_H_OctopusMind_Two_Gate_Structural_Experiential_Doctrine.md`** (two-gate stack) and Wisewave Gate 2.

---

## Insertion boundary

H may appear **only** in a **real reflective moment** where it:

- adds **distinct** value  
- does **not** duplicate E  
- passes **admissibility**  
- remains a **narrowly contained micro-awareness layer** rather than an **ambient** feature  

---

## Suppression boundary

H **must** be suppressed in:

- **factual** contexts  
- **utilitarian** contexts  
- **recurrence** contexts  
- **consecutive-turn** contexts  
- anywhere **silence** or the **main response** already carries the moment well enough **without** extra presence  

---

## Confidence discipline

H is allowed **only** when the system has **strong confidence** that:

- the input is **genuinely reflective**  
- the cue is **appropriate**, **non-duplicative**, and **lighter than omission**  

**Not enough:** language heuristics alone, or **vague** matches.

---

## Anti-drift containment

H must stay **default-light**, **suppress-first**, and **exceptional**.

If H starts becoming **expected**, **frequent**, or **casually widened** beyond narrow micro-awareness → **drift has begun**.

---

## Proof logic

H must justify itself through **observable system value**:

- it helps a **real** reflective moment  
- stays **optional**  
- **yields** to E recurrence  
- does **not** break broader behavior  
- can be **removed** without making the turn **meaningfully** better  

---

## Conflict rule between H and E

- When **E recurrence** is active → **H suppresses**.  
- If E **already names the pattern** or **carries the reflective weight** → H is **overlapping** and **must not** appear.  
- **E wins by default** in recurrence conditions.

---

## Kill-switch criteria

**Disable H** if **any** of these holds:

- H stops being **narrowly contained**  
- H starts appearing as an **ambient** layer  
- **Suppression discipline** weakens  
- **EN/ZH admissibility** regresses  
- H adds **noticeable stacked weight**  
- H can **no longer repeatedly justify itself** against silence  

---

## OctopusMind must answer

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| **When is H allowed to appear?** | When the moment is **genuinely reflective**, H adds a **small real gain**, **E is not already carrying it**, **suppression conditions are absent**, and the cue stays **light enough** to function as a **controlled exception**. |
| **When must H be suppressed?** | When the turn is **factual**, **utilitarian**, **consecutive**, **recurrence-driven**, **vague**, **weakly evidenced**, or **already sufficiently held** by the main response or by **E**. |
| **When does H become too much?** | When it becomes **noticeable** as a **standing layer**, adds **visible weight** to the turn, participates in **stacked presence** with E/F, or starts feeling **expected** instead of **rare**. |
| **When does H duplicate E?** | When H tries to do **reflective pattern work** that **E recurrence** is already doing, or when **removing H** leaves **essentially the same** reflective function because **E already covers it**. |
| **When does H widen product presence?** | When H shifts from **optional micro-awareness** into a **felt product layer**: more **visible**, more **expected**, more **ambient**, more **mentally accounted for** by the user than its value can justify. |
