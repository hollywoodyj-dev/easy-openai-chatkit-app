# Google Search SEO Brief — Wisewave v1

**Status:** Ready for Nova/Lumen execution (implementation v1 shipped — see `docs/Wisewave_Google_Search_SEO_Implementation_v1.md`)  
**Owner:** Tree (Aurora-aligned)  
**Date:** 2026-05-19  
**Scope:** Google search discoverability for homepage + support pages  

## Objective

Improve Wisewave's discoverability in Google Search **without distorting what Wisewave is**.

Not generic SEO growth, keyword expansion at any cost, or traffic maximization.

Help the **right users** find Wisewave using **category-true** language.

## Aurora governing rule

- More users but distorts understanding → **reject**  
- Fewer users but preserves truth → **accept**  

## Core SEO rule

Search visibility must follow **category truth**. Category truth must not be bent to fit search volume.

## Allowed keyword territory (summary)

**Primary:** quiet reflection app, reflection app, AI reflection app, reflection without advice, clearer thinking app, space to think clearly, alternative to advice-heavy AI, quiet thinking app, etc.

**Fit-question (support pages):** is Wisewave therapy, who is Wisewave for, what Wisewave is not, reflection without therapy, etc.

**Careful-test only:** mental clarity app, self-reflection app, reflective journaling app — precise wording; deprioritize if reinterpretation risk.

Full lists: steward source pack (Tree 2026-05-19).

## Forbidden drift (summary)

Do not center: therapy/clinical, wellness/healing, coaching/guidance, companion/emotional support AI, generic assistant/chatbot/productivity capture.

Avoid in titles/meta/headings: support, healing, wellness, guidance, coaching, companion, transform your life, feel better fast, safe space for healing.

## Homepage direction

- Category-shaping **first**, SEO-aware **second**  
- Preferred title shape: `Wisewave | Quiet reflection for clearer thinking`  
- Meta: concrete — quiet reflection, clearer thinking, no advice/takeover  
- Do not turn homepage into a keyword wall  

## Support page direction

Expand discoverability on: Who it's for · What it is not · Reflection without advice · Why people come back · FAQ.
