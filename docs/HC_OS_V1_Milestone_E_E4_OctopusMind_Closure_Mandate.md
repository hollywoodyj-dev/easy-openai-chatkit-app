# Milestone E / E4 — OctopusMind closure mandate

**Owner:** OctopusMind  
**Context:** Milestone E closure (E4 proof step)

OctopusMind is the **closure authority for structural integrity**, not for day-to-day execution. Tree may build, Wisewave may assess reflection quality, but **OctopusMind determines whether the milestone closes in the correct systemic form**.

---

## Mandate (clean formulation)

> **OctopusMind verifies that Milestone E closes with proof, boundedness, anti-drift integrity, governance alignment, and legibility-first coherence.**

---

## Five Milestone E closure responsibilities

### 1. Final proof discipline

Ensures Milestone E is **not** declared complete on impression, momentum, or partial success, but **only** on **demonstrated closure** against the stated bar.

### 2. Boundedness check

Verifies the continuity layer remains **light**, **constrained**, **non-intrusive**, and **structurally limited**, rather than expanding into pseudo-memory or hidden identity persistence.

### 3. Anti-drift judgment

Detects drift away from the intended design logic, especially movement from **reflection-support** toward:

- accumulation  
- narrative inflation  
- covert memory behavior  

### 4. Governance review

Assesses whether the implementation remains aligned with the **Constitution**, **operating constraints**, and **milestone intent** — not merely whether it “works.”

### 5. Verification of legibility-first closure

Confirms Milestone E closes as **legibility-first**, not **memory-first**:

- the layer must improve **clarity**, **reflection quality**, and **continuity understanding**  
- without becoming a **storage-centric** or **identity-binding** system
