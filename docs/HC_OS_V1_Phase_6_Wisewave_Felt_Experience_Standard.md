# Wisewave — Phase 6 Felt Experience Standard
## Continue Mechanism — Earned Trust Without Coldness

## 0. Purpose
Wisewave’s role in Phase 6 is to protect the felt experience of Continue while the system becomes more selective.

Phase 6 is not trying to make Continue more visible.
It is trying to make Continue more earned.

The risk is not only that Continue appears too often.
The equal and opposite risk is:
- suppression becomes correct, but the product starts to feel colder, thinner, or less human.

Wisewave exists to prevent that.

---

## 1. One-line governing rule

**Continue should feel more deserved when it appears, but never more withholding when it does not.**

---

## 2. Core question

Wisewave must answer one question throughout Phase 6:

**Does tighter surfacing make Continue feel more earned and trustworthy, rather than more absent or less humane?**

This is the main felt-experience test.

---

## 3. What Wisewave owns

Wisewave owns protection of:
- felt experience quality
- lightness
- trust feel
- non-intrusive re-entry quality
- gentleness
- low system presence
- low pressure
- natural return feel

Wisewave does not own:
- raw usage volume
- CTR optimization
- surfacing frequency targets
- instrumentation strategy
- system-object expansion

Wisewave is not here to make Continue bigger.
Wisewave is here to make it feel right.

---

## 4. The desired felt outcome

When Phase 6 tuning is working, the user should feel:

### When Continue appears
- “this makes sense here”
- “this is timely”
- “this feels lightly helpful”
- “this seems to know when not to get in the way”

### When Continue does not appear
- “nothing is missing”
- “the space still feels clean”
- “I can just continue normally”

The user should not feel:

### When Continue appears
- “this is always here”
- “this UI wants attention”
- “the system is trying too hard”
- “this feels like product machinery”

### When Continue does not appear
- “something that should have helped is gone”
- “the system feels colder now”
- “it is withholding on purpose”
- “the experience feels less humane”

---

## 5. Felt experience success standard

Phase 6 is succeeding only if all of the following become more true:

### A. Earned appearance
When Continue appears, it feels deserved rather than ambient.

### B. Lower decorative feel
Continue feels less like UI furniture.

### C. Maintained gentleness
Stronger suppression does not make the system feel rigid or emotionally flat.

### D. Trust through restraint
Selective absence increases trust instead of reducing warmth.

### E. Natural re-entry
When Continue is used, the user still feels they can re-enter with minimal effort.

This directly matches the broader Phase 6 intention: improve selectivity without making Continue heavier, more visible, or more system-like.

---

## 6. Wisewave warning signs

Wisewave should treat the following as felt-experience warning signs:

### Warning 1 — Coldness drift
Suppression gets tighter, but the product begins to feel:
- less welcoming
- less alive
- more “empty by design”

### Warning 2 — Over-withholding
Continue disappears from moments where it would have gently helped.

### Warning 3 — Mechanical selectivity
The system becomes correct in logic but loses softness in timing.

### Warning 4 — Decorative survival
Continue still appears in weak cases and feels like optional furniture.

### Warning 5 — Trust erosion through unpredictability
The user cannot tell whether Continue will genuinely help when it appears.

### Warning 6 — Re-entry friction
After clicking, the user still feels they need to rebuild too much context.

---

## 7. Wisewave evaluation dimensions

Wisewave should evaluate Phase 6 tuning across five dimensions.

### 7.1 Earnedness
Question:
When Continue appears, does it feel like it belongs here?

Pass if:
- appearance feels timely
- user could plausibly think “yes, this helps here”

Fail if:
- appearance feels habitual
- appearance feels generic
- appearance feels too easy to ignore because it feels decorative

### 7.2 Gentleness
Question:
Does the system still feel soft enough to return to?

Pass if:
- the space still feels easy to enter
- suppression does not create harsh emptiness

Fail if:
- selective absence makes the space feel emotionally colder
- Continue starts feeling rationed rather than natural

### 7.3 Trust feel
Question:
Does selectivity increase confidence?

Pass if:
- the user can increasingly trust that visible Continue is worth considering

Fail if:
- user starts to see Continue as inconsistent
- Continue feels arbitrary in either presence or absence

### 7.4 Low system presence
Question:
Does Continue remain a light affordance rather than a visible concept?

Pass if:
- it still feels like a gentle carry-over cue

Fail if:
- tuning creates stronger system visibility
- user can feel the machinery more than before

### 7.5 Non-intrusive re-entry
Question:
When Continue is used, does the return still feel natural?

Pass if:
- user can continue with low-verbal re-entry
- resumed reply feels lightly held, not over-anchored

Fail if:
- resumption feels forced
- selected direction feels too imposed
- user must work to reconnect anyway

---

## 8. Healthy felt pattern

Wisewave should recognize this as the healthy Phase 6 pattern:

### Pattern
- Continue appears less often
- but when it appears, it feels more relevant
- user trusts it more
- ignore remains easy
- the product does not feel colder
- strong re-entry moments still feel easy and humane

In other words:

**less frequent, more believable, equally gentle**

---

## 9. Unhealthy felt pattern

Wisewave should flag this as unhealthy:

### Pattern
- Continue appears less often
- but the product feels more barren
- strong moments are missed
- the space feels more sterile
- return feels less natural
- the system seems logically improved but experientially thinned out

In other words:

**better suppression, worse humanity**

That is not a Phase 6 success.

---

## 10. Wisewave pass / hold criteria

### Pass Phase 6 from Wisewave side if:
- Continue feels more earned when present
- decorative feel is lower
- strong useful moments still feel gently supported
- ignore remains clean
- the product does not feel colder, harsher, or more withholding
- re-entry still feels natural and low-pressure

### Hold Phase 6 from Wisewave side if:
- suppression gains come with felt coldness
- Continue starts to feel too rare in moments of real need
- the space loses softness
- resumed re-entry feels less humane
- trust does not actually increase with selectivity

---

## 11. Wisewave operating bias

Wisewave should use this bias during review:

**Prefer earned absence over decorative presence.**

But reject earned absence if it becomes experiential coldness.

This is the balancing rule.

---

## 12. Questions Wisewave must answer in review

For each major tuning pass, Wisewave should answer:
- Does Continue now feel more deserved when visible?
- Does reduced surfacing improve trust or merely reduce noise?
- Are strong emotional / low-verbal / unfinished moments still supported well?
- Does the space still feel gentle to return to?
- Has tighter suppression made the system feel more absent in a bad way?
- Does Continue still feel like a soft carry-over cue rather than visible machinery?

---

## 13. Recommended Wisewave review output format

Wisewave should return review notes in this structure:

### A. Earned appearance read
Does Continue feel more justified when it appears?

### B. Gentleness read
Has stronger selectivity preserved softness?

### C. Trust read
Does appearance now feel more credible?

### D. Re-entry read
Does Continue still help users come back in naturally?

### E. Coldness / absence risk
Has any tuning made the system feel thinner, harsher, or emotionally less available?

### F. Overall judgment
- pass
- pass with watchpoints
- hold

---

## 14. Final governing line

A good Phase 6 does not make Continue merely rarer.
It makes Continue more trustworthy while keeping the return space gentle.

---

## 15. One-line summary for Tree / Nova / Lumen

Wisewave standard for Phase 6:

**Continue should feel more earned when present,
less decorative overall,
and no less humane as selective suppression increases.**
